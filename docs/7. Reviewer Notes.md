```txt
-- P(3 | Made): given that a shot went in, was it a three? --
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  P(3|Made) = P(Made|3) * P(3) / P(Made)
  Step 1: P(Made|3) = 0.418575
  Step 2: P(3)      = 0.548117
  Step 3: P(Made|3) * P(3) = 0.229428   <- P(Made AND 3)
  Step 4: P(Made)   = 0.490237
  Step 5: P(3|Made) = 0.229428 / 0.490237 = 0.467994
  Result: 0.4680  (46.80%)
```
